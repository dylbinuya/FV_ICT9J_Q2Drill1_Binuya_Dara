How to Play Badminton

Objective of the Game

• Score Points: Land the shuttlecock on the opponent's side.
• Force Errors: Make your opponent hit the shuttle out or into the net.
• Win the Match: Reach 21 points first and win the best of three games.

Basic Rules

• Scoring: A point is scored after every rally.
• Serving: The serve must travel diagonally into the opponent's service box.
• Faults: You lose the point if the shuttle goes out or into the net.

Common Mistakes

• Gripping Too Tight: This can tire your arm quickly.
• Hitting Too Late: This can make your shots less effective.
• Standing Flat-Footed: This makes it harder to move quickly.
